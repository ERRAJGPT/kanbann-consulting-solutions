Why it matters

The first days set the tone for everything that follows.

The stretch between 'yes' and day one is when a new hire is quietly deciding whether they made the right choice. Slow paperwork or an awkward silence can undo all the excitement they felt. Warm, precise, well-timed onboarding tells someone they're genuinely wanted — and starts the relationship on trust.

  • New hires left wondering, and going quiet before their first day
  • Offer letters and contracts that come across as cold or careless
  • People arriving to confusion instead of a warm welcome
