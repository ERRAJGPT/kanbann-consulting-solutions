Why it matters

People do their best work when they can see why it matters.

Vague goals leave people busy but unsure they're making a difference, and once-a-year reviews rarely help anyone grow. When everyone can see how their work connects to something bigger — and gets honest, kind, regular feedback — work becomes more meaningful, and performance follows naturally.

  • People working hard without a clear sense of what it adds up to
  • Reviews that feel like a formality rather than real support
  • Teams pulling in different directions without meaning to
