Why it matters

Everyone wants to feel that their effort is seen.

When recognition is rare or reserved for the loudest, the quiet people who hold things together can start to feel invisible — and eventually, they leave. Appreciation, given genuinely and consistently, is one of the most human things a workplace can offer. We help you make it part of how you work.

  • Dedicated people quietly feeling their effort goes unnoticed
  • Recognition that's sporadic, political, or only ever about money
  • Losing good people who simply didn't feel appreciated
